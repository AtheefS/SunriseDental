Sunrise Dental Clinic

Appointment & Patient Management System

Help & User Guide

1. Login

Enter your authorized username and password on the login page and click the Login button.

2. Register New Appointment

From the Dashboard, select "Register New Appointment". Enter the patient's name, address, contact number, dentist, treatment, appointment date and appointment time. Then click "Register Appointment".

3. Search Appointment

Select "Search Appointment" from the Dashboard. Enter the appointment number and click "Search Appointment" to view the complete appointment details.

4. Calculate & Print Bill

Select "Calculate & Print Bill" from the Dashboard. Enter the appointment number and click "Calculate Bill". The system calculates the consultation fee and treatment cost and displays the total bill. Click "Print Bill" to print the receipt.

5. Reports

Open the Reports section to generate the daily appointment report and view the billing and revenue summary.

6. Logout

Click "Logout" from the Dashboard after completing your work. This safely returns you to the login page.

Important

Always check the appointment date, time, dentist and patient information before registering an appointment.
